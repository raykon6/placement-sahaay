Evaluation order

Every rule runs and is reported; the most severe outcome decides

  1. R1Record statusArchived records cannot participate.
  2. R2RegistrationOnly students registered for the session may participate; deregistered students are blocked.
  3. R3EligibilityEligibility must be verified; pending verification is referred for review.
  4. R4Company criteriaProgramme must be eligible for the drive and CGPA must meet any minimum.
  5. R5AttendanceAn unexcused absence at any recruitment event bars the next {{ s.absence_debar_drives }} eligible drives. Officers may record emergency exceptions with a reason.
  6. R6Offer typeI / F / I+F restrictions above.
  7. R7Category switchingA+ / A / B / C rules above, based on the best FTE offer held.
