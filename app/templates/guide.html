How Placement Sahaay works

A short tour of every module: what it does, who can use it, and what happens behind the scenes.

The placement journey

  1. 0. Self-registration. Students apply with their enrollment number and official e-mail (each unique on the platform), optionally verify the e-mail with a one-time code, and their programme coordinator approves the request. Approval creates the record and the login.
  2. 1. Register and import. The Placement Cell adds students one by one or imports a CSV. Every field is validated on the server, and duplicate Placement IDs, mobile numbers and emails are rejected.
  3. 2. Verify eligibility. Course coordinators confirm academic eligibility and CGPA for their own programme.
  4. 3. Run drives. Officers create drives. Students apply from their portal, or officers register them. Each application passes the rule engine first.
  5. 4. Record attendance. Attendance is recorded for every event. An unexcused absence bars the student from the next {{ s.absence_debar_drives }} eligible drives unless an officer records an emergency exception.
  6. 5. Record offers. The server works out the category and the acceptance deadline. If an offer breaks policy, it is still recorded but flagged for the Placement Cell.
  7. 6. Report and audit. Dashboards update live. Exports are controlled, and every sensitive action can be traced in the audit trail.

The rule engine

Runs on every application, registration, offer and simulation

Every check runs and reports its reason. The most severe outcome decides: Blocked beats Manual review, which beats Allowed.

  • R1RecordArchived records cannot take part.
  • R2RegistrationOnly students registered for the session can take part.
  • R3EligibilityIneligible students are blocked; pending verification goes to review.
  • R4Company criteriaThe student's programme must be eligible and their CGPA must meet the drive's minimum.
  • R5AttendanceActive absence ban → blocked.
  • R6Offer typeI → no other I. F → no other F. I+F (or an accepted PPO) → no roles at other organisations.
  • R7SwitchingA+ → out of the process. A → A+ only. B → at least {{ s.b_switch_multiplier }}× the current package. C → higher categories only.

If the offer-type rule and the switching rule disagree (an FTE holder upgrading to another FTE-only role), the case is referred to the Placement Cell by default. The administrator can change this under Policy settings.

Security at a glance

  • Sign-in. Passwords are hashed with scrypt. Optional authenticator-app codes. Lockout after 5 failures.
  • Sessions. End after 15 minutes idle; revoked at sign-out; tied to your browser.
  • Least privilege. Each role sees only its scope, and the server checks this on every request.
  • Encryption. Mobile numbers and emails are stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM).
  • Forms. Validated on the server; protected against SQL injection, scripts and forged requests.
  • Audit. Append-only and tamper-evident.
  • Backups. Encrypted and test-restorable.
